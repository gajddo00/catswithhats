## What is it?

Cats with Hats is an iOS app where you collect cats wearing different hats. Inspired by Japanese gachapon vending machines — a concept one of the team brought back from a recent trip to Japan — you spend tokens to unlock new cats and grow your collection. Simple, silly, and fun, with bold colors, playful animations, and a design that's hard to look away from.

## How it was made
 
The app was put together in ~3 hours during a single collaborative session at the hackathon. The team brainstormed ideas, assigned tasks, and shipped — all under serious time pressure.
